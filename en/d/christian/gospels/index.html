Moments from the Gospels

Giving, sharing, and a life turned around.

  1. 1

    The Widow's Two Coins

    Jesus sat down opposite the place in the temple where offerings were put, and he watched the crowd putting in their money.

  2. 2

    A Boy's Five Loaves

    A great crowd followed Jesus to the far side of the Sea of Galilee, because they had seen him heal the sick.

  3. 3

    The Man in the Sycamore Tree

    In Jericho lived a man named Zacchaeus, a chief tax collector. He was very rich, and people despised him, for he had grown wealthy by cheating them.

These stories are shared with equal respect for every faith. Any images on this site exist only to help tell a story, with reverence; in the Islam section no one's face is ever shown.
