Stories of the Old Testament

Job and Ruth.

  1. 1

    The Trial of Job

    In the land of Uz lived a man named Job. He was blameless and upright, he feared God and turned away from evil. He had seven sons, three daughters, and great wealth.

  2. 2

    Ruth Stays with Naomi

    When famine struck Bethlehem, a woman named Naomi went with her husband and two sons to the land of Moab. Her sons married women of Moab, Orpah and Ruth.

These stories are shared with equal respect for every faith. Any images on this site exist only to help tell a story, with reverence; in the Islam section no one's face is ever shown.
