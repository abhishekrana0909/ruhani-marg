Parables of Jesus

Stories Jesus told to teach love and mercy.

  1. 1

    The Good Samaritan

    An expert in the law asked Jesus, "Who is my neighbour?" Jesus answered with a story.

  2. 2

    The Son Who Came Home

    A man had two sons. The younger said, "Father, give me my share of the property now." So the father divided what he had between them.

  3. 3

    The One Lost Sheep

    People were grumbling that Jesus welcomed sinners and ate with them. So he told them a story.

These stories are shared with equal respect for every faith. Any images on this site exist only to help tell a story, with reverence; in the Islam section no one's face is ever shown.
