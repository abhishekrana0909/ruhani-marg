Lives of the Saints

Saint Martin and Saint Francis.

  1. 1

    Saint Martin's Cloak

    Martin was a young soldier in the Roman army, posted at the city of Amiens in France. He was preparing to become a Christian but had not yet been baptised.

  2. 2

    Saint Francis Gives Everything Back

    Francis grew up in Assisi, in Italy, the son of a rich cloth merchant named Pietro Bernardone. He loved fine clothes, songs and parties, and he dreamed of glory as a knight.

These stories are shared with equal respect for every faith. Any images on this site exist only to help tell a story, with reverence; in the Islam section no one's face is ever shown.
