Baba Farid (RA)

Hazrat Fariduddin Masud Ganj-e-Shakar.

  1. 1

    The Sugar Under the Prayer Mat

    In the village of Kothewal, near Multan, a boy named Farid was growing up. His mother was a pious woman who wanted one thing above all: that her son should love to pray.

  2. 2

    Not Scissors, a Needle

    People from far and near came to see Baba Farid (RA) at Pakpattan. Rich and poor alike sat on the same floor of his humble khanqah.

These stories are shared with equal respect for every faith. Any images on this site exist only to help tell a story, with reverence; in the Islam section no one's face is ever shown.
