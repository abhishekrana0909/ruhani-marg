    Why He Is Called Garib Nawaz

    Hazrat Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti (RA) lost his parents as a boy. From them he inherited a small garden, and he tended it with his own hands.

These stories are shared with equal respect for every faith. Any images on this site exist only to help tell a story, with reverence; in the Islam section no one's face is ever shown.
