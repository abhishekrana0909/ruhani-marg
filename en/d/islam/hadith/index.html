Stories from the Hadith

Stories from the authentic Hadith.

  1. 1

    The Man and the Thirsty Dog

    The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ once told his companions about a man who was walking on a road and was overcome by thirst.

  2. 2

    Hazrat Hajar (AS) and the Water of Zamzam

    Hazrat Ibrahim (AS) brought his wife Hazrat Hajar (AS) and their infant son Hazrat Ismail (AS) to a valley where no one lived and there was no water. It was the place that would become Makkah.

These stories are shared with equal respect for every faith. Any images on this site exist only to help tell a story, with reverence; in the Islam section no one's face is ever shown.
