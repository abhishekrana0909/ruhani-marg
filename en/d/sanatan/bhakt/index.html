Stories of the Devotees

Prahlad, Dhruv and Sudama.

  1. 1

    Prahlad and Lord Narasimha

    Hiranyakashipu, king of the Daityas, won a mighty boon through fierce penance. No man or beast could kill him, not by day or night, not indoors or outdoors, not on the earth or in the sky.

  2. 2

    Dhruv, the Child Who Became a Star

    King Uttanapada had two queens, Suniti and Suruchi. The king loved Suruchi more. Suniti's son was little Dhruv, only five years old.

  3. 3

    Sudama's Handful of Rice

    There lived a poor Brahmin, a childhood friend of Shri Krishna. He had almost nothing, yet he was content. His wife, seeing their hunger, gently said, "Your friend is the Lord of Dwarka. Go and see him."

  4. 4

    King Rantideva's Last Drop of Water

    King Rantideva gave away whatever came to him. He kept nothing for tomorrow, and in time he and his family had nothing left at all.

These stories are shared with equal respect for every faith. Any images on this site exist only to help tell a story, with reverence; in the Islam section no one's face is ever shown.
