Stories of the Ramayana

Hanuman ji, Mata Shabari and the son who served his parents.

  1. 1

    Hanuman Ji Finds Sita Mata

    Ravana had carried Sita Mata away to Lanka, across the sea. No one in the vanar army could cross that water, until Jambavan reminded Hanuman Ji of his own strength.

  2. 2

    Mata Shabari's Long Wait

    Near Lake Pampa stood the ashram of Sage Matanga. An old woman named Shabari served there for many years, tending the place with love.

  3. 3

    The Boy at the River Sarayu

    After Shri Ram left for the forest, King Dasharatha could not sleep. One night, in his grief, he told Queen Kausalya of something he had done long ago as a young prince.

These stories are shared with equal respect for every faith. Any images on this site exist only to help tell a story, with reverence; in the Islam section no one's face is ever shown.
