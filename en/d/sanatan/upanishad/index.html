Stories of the Upanishads

The truthful boy Satyakama.

  1. 1

    Satyakama, the Boy Who Told the Truth

    A boy named Satyakama wished to study the sacred knowledge. He went to his mother, Jabala, and asked, "Mother, what is my family line? My teacher will ask me."

These stories are shared with equal respect for every faith. Any images on this site exist only to help tell a story, with reverence; in the Islam section no one's face is ever shown.
