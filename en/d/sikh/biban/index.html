Great Sikh Women

Bibi Rajni and Mata Khivi.

  1. 1

    Bibi Rajni and the Ber Tree

    Rai Duni Chand was a wealthy revenue collector in Patti. One day he asked his daughters who provided for them. The older ones praised their father.

  2. 2

    Mata Khivi's Kitchen

    Mata Khivi Ji was the wife of Guru Angad Dev Ji. When her husband left everything to serve Guru Nanak Dev Ji, she cared for their home and children on her own, without complaint.

These stories are shared with equal respect for every faith. Any images on this site exist only to help tell a story, with reverence; in the Islam section no one's face is ever shown.
