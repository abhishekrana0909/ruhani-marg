    The Five Beloved Ones

  2. 2

    Pir Budhu Shah: A Muslim Friend of the Guru

    In the town of Sadhaura lived a respected Muslim Sufi named Pir Budhu Shah. He travelled to meet the young Guru Gobind Singh Ji at Paonta, and the two became friends.

These stories are shared with equal respect for every faith. Any images on this site exist only to help tell a story, with reverence; in the Islam section no one's face is ever shown.
