Guru Nanak Dev Ji

Sakhis from the life of the first Guru.

  1. 1

    Sacha Sauda: The True Bargain

    Young Guru Nanak Dev Ji cared little for trade. His father, Mehta Kalu, worried about his future and gave him twenty rupees. "Go to the market. Make a profitable bargain."

  2. 2

    Tera, Tera: All Is Yours

    At Sultanpur Lodhi, Guru Nanak Dev Ji took charge of the government storehouse of the governor, Daulat Khan Lodi. People came to him every day for grain.

  3. 3

    Bhai Lalo's Bread

    In Saidpur, now Eminabad, lived Bhai Lalo, a poor carpenter who earned every coin by honest work. Guru Nanak Dev Ji stayed in his humble home.

  4. 4

    Sajjan: The Man Who Looked Holy

    At Tulamba near Multan stood an inn run by a man named Sajjan. He wore holy marks, kept a place of worship, and welcomed every traveller with great warmth.

  5. 5

    The Bundle of Wet Grass

    Bhai Lehna was a devout man who led pilgrims every year. One morning he heard a Sikh reciting the words of Guru Nanak Dev Ji, and his heart would not rest.

These stories are shared with equal respect for every faith. Any images on this site exist only to help tell a story, with reverence; in the Islam section no one's face is ever shown.
