The Patience of Prophet Ayyub (AS)

Story 1 / 11 min readFrom the original text

The lesson

Patience is not silence. It is telling your pain to the One who can heal it, and to no one else.

Source

The Holy Quran, Surah Al-Anbiya 21:83-84 and Surah Sad 38:41-44. The verses are given here in plain paraphrase.

The Quran gives this story briefly, so nothing has been added to it from later accounts.

Marked as read. Good night.
