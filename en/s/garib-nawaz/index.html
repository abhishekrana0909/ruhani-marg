Why He Is Called Garib Nawaz

Story 1 / 11 min readAccording to tradition

The lesson

Be like the river, the sun and the earth: they do not ask who you are before they give.

Source

According to tradition, from the Chishti accounts of the Khwaja's life. The saying of the three qualities is widely attributed to him.

Reliable early records of his life are few, and the wording of the saying varies.
