Hazrat Hajar (AS) and the Water of Zamzam

Story 2 / 22 min readFrom the original text

The lesson

Trust in Allah, and still run between the hills. Faith and effort walk together.

Source

Sahih al-Bukhari 3364, narrated by Ibn Abbas (RA). Given here in plain paraphrase.

Marked as read. Good night.
