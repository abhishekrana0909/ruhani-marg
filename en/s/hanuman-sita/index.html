Hanuman Ji Finds Sita Mata

Story 1 / 31 min readFrom the original text

The lesson

Even the strongest sometimes need reminding of who they are. Then, do not stop searching.

Source

Valmiki Ramayana, Sundara Kanda (the ring is given in Sarga 36); Jambavan's reminder comes at the end of Kishkindha Kanda.

Marked as read. Good night.
