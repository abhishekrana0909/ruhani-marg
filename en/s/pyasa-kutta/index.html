The Man and the Thirsty Dog

Story 1 / 21 min readFrom the original text

The lesson

No kindness is too small, not even a shoe full of water for a dog.

Source

Sahih al-Bukhari 2363 and Sahih Muslim 2244, narrated by Abu Hurairah (RA).
