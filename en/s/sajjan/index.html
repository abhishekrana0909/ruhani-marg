Sajjan: The Man Who Looked Holy

Story 4 / 51 min readAccording to tradition

The lesson

A clean outside means nothing until the heart is clean. And no one is too far gone to change.

Source

Janamsakhi tradition; the shabad is in Guru Granth Sahib, Ang 729 (Raag Suhi, Mahalla 1). The verse above is a paraphrase.

Marked as read. Good night.
