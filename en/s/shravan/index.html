The Boy at the River Sarayu

Story 3 / 31 min readFrom the original text

The lesson

Our parents' thirst comes before our own. And every hasty act has a long echo.

Source

Valmiki Ramayana, Ayodhya Kanda, Sargas 63 and 64.

Later tradition names this boy Shravan Kumar; Valmiki's text does not give his name.

Marked as read. Good night.
