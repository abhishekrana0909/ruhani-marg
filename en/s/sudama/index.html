Sudama's Handful of Rice

Story 3 / 41 min readFrom the original text

The lesson

A small gift given with love weighs more than any treasure.

Source

Bhagavata Purana, Canto 10, Chapters 80 and 81.

The name Sudama comes from tradition; the verses themselves mostly call him Krishna's Brahmin friend.

Marked as read. Good night.
