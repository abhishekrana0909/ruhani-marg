Giving

Choose a lesson and read how every faith teaches it.

ॐ

Sanatan Dharma

  1. 1

    King Rantideva's Last Drop of Water

    King Rantideva gave away whatever came to him. He kept nothing for tomorrow, and in time he and his family had nothing left at all.

  2. 2

    Sudama's Handful of Rice

    There lived a poor Brahmin, a childhood friend of Shri Krishna. He had almost nothing, yet he was content. His wife, seeing their hunger, gently said, "Your friend is the Lord of Dwarka. Go and see him."

ੴ

Sikhism

  1. 1

    Sacha Sauda: The True Bargain

    Young Guru Nanak Dev Ji cared little for trade. His father, Mehta Kalu, worried about his future and gave him twenty rupees. "Go to the market. Make a profitable bargain."

  2. 2

    Tera, Tera: All Is Yours

    At Sultanpur Lodhi, Guru Nanak Dev Ji took charge of the government storehouse of the governor, Daulat Khan Lodi. People came to him every day for grain.

Islam

  1. 1

    Why He Is Called Garib Nawaz

    Hazrat Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti (RA) lost his parents as a boy. From them he inherited a small garden, and he tended it with his own hands.

Christianity

  1. 1

    The Widow's Two Coins

    Jesus sat down opposite the place in the temple where offerings were put, and he watched the crowd putting in their money.

  2. 2

    A Boy's Five Loaves

    A great crowd followed Jesus to the far side of the Sea of Galilee, because they had seen him heal the sick.
