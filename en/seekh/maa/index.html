Sanatan Dharma

    The Boy at the River Sarayu

Sikhism

  1. 1

    Mata Khivi's Kitchen

    Mata Khivi Ji was the wife of Guru Angad Dev Ji. When her husband left everything to serve Guru Nanak Dev Ji, she cared for their home and children on her own, without complaint.

Islam

  1. 1

    The Day the Ox Spoke

    Hazrat Shaikh Abdul Qadir Jilani (RA), known with love as Ghaus Pak, grew up in Jilan. Tradition says that when he was about eighteen, he went out to the fields on the Day of Arafah.

  2. 2

    The Sugar Under the Prayer Mat

    In the village of Kothewal, near Multan, a boy named Farid was growing up. His mother was a pious woman who wanted one thing above all: that her son should love to pray.

  3. 3

    Hazrat Hajar (AS) and the Water of Zamzam

    Hazrat Ibrahim (AS) brought his wife Hazrat Hajar (AS) and their infant son Hazrat Ismail (AS) to a valley where no one lived and there was no water. It was the place that would become Makkah.

Christianity

  1. 1

    Ruth Stays with Naomi

    When famine struck Bethlehem, a woman named Naomi went with her husband and two sons to the land of Moab. Her sons married women of Moab, Orpah and Ruth.
