Sanatan Dharma

    Satyakama, the Boy Who Told the Truth

    A boy named Satyakama wished to study the sacred knowledge. He went to his mother, Jabala, and asked, "Mother, what is my family line? My teacher will ask me."

Sikhism

    Bhai Lalo's Bread

    In Saidpur, now Eminabad, lived Bhai Lalo, a poor carpenter who earned every coin by honest work. Guru Nanak Dev Ji stayed in his humble home.

Islam

    The Forty Dinars

    Before he left for Baghdad, his mother sewed forty dinars into his garment. It was his share of what his father had left.

    The Man in the Sycamore Tree

    In Jericho lived a man named Zacchaeus, a chief tax collector. He was very rich, and people despised him, for he had grown wealthy by cheating them.
